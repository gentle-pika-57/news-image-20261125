25 NOVEMBER 2026
WEDNESDAY  ·  ONE DAY'S WORLD NEWS, IN ONE PICTURE
1
SPACE  ·  JIUQUAN  ·  ①

PAKISTAN'S FIRST ASTRONAUT REACHES TIANGONG

Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, launched Wednesday at 08:18 Beijing time aboard Shenzhou-24 — the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut aboard China's Tiangong station. He will spend about a week running Pakistani experiments.

2
MARKETS  ·  NEW YORK

AI STOCKS PLUNGE AS TARIFF THREAT RETURNS

A day after Nvidia surged 31.5%, the AI trade collapsed: Nasdaq fell 1.5%, S&P 500 0.5%, Nvidia about 6%, Micron 11% and the chip index about 8%. The Dow rose 0.8%.

3
TECH  ·  BROOKLYN

HUAWEI GUILTY OF TRADE-SECRET THEFT

A US jury convicted Huawei on 10 of 12 counts — conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud — but acquitted it of racketeering. The case began in 2018 over its Iran business.

4
CHINA  ·  SHANGHAI

CHINA PROBES TOP LEADER CHEN JINING

Beijing announced a corruption probe into Politburo member and Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, 62 — the fourth Politburo member to fall since 2025.

5
SECURITY  ·  WASHINGTON

CIA BREACH EXPOSES UNCLASSIFIED DATA

The CIA and the director of national intelligence's office recently found a cybersecurity breach exposing some unclassified information, the New York Times reported Tuesday.

6
WHITE HOUSE  ·  WASHINGTON

TRUMP PARDONS TWO THANKSGIVING TURKEYS

President Trump pardoned turkeys Freedom and Feathers in the 79th annual ceremony. The birds, hatched 15 July and weighing 82 lb combined, retire to Texas A&M. Feathers wandered off mid-ceremony.

7
LABOUR  ·  GERMANY

400,000 GERMANS WALK OUT IN STRIKE

More than 400,000 workers have joined IG Metall's strike wave across Germany, with 24-hour stoppages halting car plants as the union demands a 5% pay rise.

8
CRISIS  ·  GAZA

FLOODS HIT TENS OF THOUSANDS IN GAZA

Deadly floods swamped tens of thousands in Gaza, deepening the humanitarian crisis. An Israeli strike in Gaza City also killed Hamas security chief Naeem Abu Nahel.

9
WAR  ·  GULF OF FINLAND / LIBYA

RUSSIA BLAMES UKRAINE FOR SHIP DRONE STRIKES

Moscow accused Kyiv of “terrorist acts” after drones hit a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland and a warship off Benghazi — days after Ukraine sank the corvette Merkury.

10
QUAKE  ·  NEPAL

5.6 QUAKE HITS EASTERN NEPAL

A magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck Koshi province near the Tibet border at 07:17 local time on Tuesday, shaking the region near the border.

11
SPORT  ·  LAUSANNE

SENEGAL REGAINS AFCON CROWN AFTER CAS RULING

Senegal won back the Africa Cup of Nations title on Tuesday, more than 10 months after a chaotic final, as CAS overturned the ruling that had handed the trophy to Morocco.
